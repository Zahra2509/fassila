How to play

Guess the song. Flip the CD. Crack the code.

  1. 01
    Scan and pick a nickname

    Your phone shows your own row of 10 hidden CDs.

  2. 02
    Listen, then guess

    A few seconds of a song. Type the title: one guess per song, 30 seconds after the clip ends.

  3. 03
    The answer is revealed

    Your guess is checked automatically. Small typos are fine. Right answers flip one of your CDs.

  4. 04
    Final round

    Tap a hidden CD and guess its letter. Right letters flip too.

  5. 05
    Crack the code

    Flip all 10 CDs to reveal the secret message and win.
